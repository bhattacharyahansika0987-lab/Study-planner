🧠 Proven Study Techniques Hub

1. Pomodoro Technique 🍅

Work with intense focus for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute rejuvenating break. Keeps burnout away!

2. Feynman Technique 💡

Study a concept, then explain it out loud in simple terms as if teaching a beginner. Instantly exposes knowledge gaps.

3. Active Recall & Spaced Repetition 📝

Test yourself instead of passively re-reading notes. Review difficult topics at increasing intervals.

4. The Blurting Method 🧠

Read a section, close your book, and "blurt" out everything you remember on a blank sheet, then check for missing details.
