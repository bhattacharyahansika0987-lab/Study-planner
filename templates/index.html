🧠 Study Techniques with Built-In Timers

1. Pomodoro Technique 🍅

25 mins of deep focus, then a 5 min rest. Repeat and take a long break after four rounds.

2. Feynman Technique 💡

Explain a concept out loud in simple words, as if teaching a friend. Gaps in your explanation show what to review.

3. The Blurting Method 🧠

Write everything you remember on a blank page, then check your notes and fill in what you missed.
